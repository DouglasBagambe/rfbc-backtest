# GOAT Stride / Instant HERO rule verification

Checked 14 October 2026 against official GOAT documentation. This is not confirmation of account-specific dashboard floors or approval of the proposed automation.

| Rule | Official evidence and engineering consequence |
|---|---|
| Exact model | [Stride](https://help.goatfundedtrader.com/en/articles/17333437-goat-stride-model) identifies a $2,500 account using HERO rules; dashboard label Instant Hero is expected. |
| Daily loss | Stride states 3%, resetting at 5 PM EST from balance or equity at reset. Starting-day basis needs evidence. HERO wording differs about daily versus initial balance. Whether EST means fixed UTC−5 or New York civil time is not explicitly resolved. Missing reset evidence blocks real signals. |
| Maximum loss | Stride describes an equity peak multiplied by 95%, increasing as equity rises and never decreasing; resets after payout. No initial-balance cap appears in that rule. The draft's capped, fixed-dollar formula was removed. HERO also references EOD in a separate paragraph, leaving sampling ambiguity. Historical peak and payout reset cannot be reconstructed from first observation. |
| Floating loss | Combined floating loss reaching 1% of balance closes the account. Independently enforced alongside daily and maximum loss. At a $2,500 balance this is $25; at $2,476.17 it is $24.7617. Fees and swaps count in rule calculations. |
| Profitable days | Six days earning at least 0.5% of initial balance, $12.50 per qualifying day. |
| Consistency | Stride permits a highest profit day at exactly 15%; [HERO](https://help.goatfundedtrader.com/en/articles/16097387-instant-hero-model) says below 15%. Treat equality as unresolved. Payout eligibility is distinct from an account breach. |
| Leverage | Stride says up to 1:100; HERO lists instrument-class leverage. Use broker margin calculations; do not infer leverage for execution. Stride cannot be reset or merged. |
| EAs | [EA policy](https://help.goatfundedtrader.com/en/articles/10749630-can-i-use-expert-advisors-eas), updated 12 October, allows only self-developed EAs, with ownership proof on request. Purchased/shared EAs, HFT, Gold Arbitrage EAs and third-party trade copiers are prohibited. Custom source ownership alone does not establish approval of hosted decisions. |
| Hosting | [Hosting policy](https://help.goatfundedtrader.com/en/articles/17362726-can-i-use-a-vpn-vps-proxy-or-any-other-hosting-service-while-trading), effective 6 October, prohibits VPN/VPS/proxy/remote/hosted or routing services during trading. Only first-login VPN connection is excepted. Whether locally executed orders directed by Render fall within this prohibition requires explicit GOAT clarification. No permissive assumption is made. |
| Hedging | [Hedging policy](https://help.goatfundedtrader.com/en/articles/10742101-is-hedging-allowed) prohibits opposite positions on the same asset, irrespective of volume/risk, within and across accounts, including other firms. This terminal cannot see other accounts. Hedge account mode is a broker capability, not GOAT permission. |
| Signals | [Signals policy](https://help.goatfundedtrader.com/en/articles/15945071-copy-trading-and-signals), updated 12 October, prohibits third-party signals and external-account copying; only the GOAT Trade Copier supports copying among one's own GOAT accounts. Account-holder decision independence is required. G_DESK/ChatGPT and RFBC ownership, decision generation and hosting need clarification before live automation. |
| News | [News policy](https://help.goatfundedtrader.com/en/articles/10742084-is-news-trading-allowed) permits news trading but removes per-trade profits exceeding 1% initial balance for opens/closes within five minutes either side of high-impact releases. Includes SL/TP and pending fills. Authoritative calendar synchronization remains unimplemented. |
| Weekend/overnight | [Weekend holding](https://help.goatfundedtrader.com/en/articles/10742082-can-i-hold-trades-over-the-weekend) is allowed. No separate overnight prohibition was found in the reviewed rules; this is not account-specific confirmation. [Gap policy](https://help.goatfundedtrader.com/en/articles/14123389-do-you-allow-trading-the-weekend-gap) reviews last-three-hours-Friday/first-three-hours-Monday trades and removes affected funded profits. |
| Holding time | [Minimum holding time](https://help.goatfundedtrader.com/en/articles/12849041-what-is-the-minimum-holding-time-for-trades): 120 seconds for purchases before 6 October 2026 00:00 UTC, otherwise 60 seconds. Early profits are removed; losses remain. Purchase date has not been verified. Emergency loss protection must not be delayed to preserve payout eligibility. |
| Other prohibited practices | [Prohibited practices](https://help.goatfundedtrader.com/en/articles/10742118-what-are-prohibited-trading-practices): latency/error exploitation, insider trading, front running, all-or-nothing risk, prohibited third-party strategies, martingale/recovery grids and simulated-environment abuse. Broker/firm discretion can impose further restrictions. |
| Account-specific restrictions | [Risk limitation policy](https://help.goatfundedtrader.com/en/articles/10742114-risk-limitation-policy) allows imposed per-idea limits. Dashboard notices are not available to this bridge. [Trading day](https://help.goatfundedtrader.com/en/articles/12687827-what-time-does-a-trading-day-start) references 5 PM EST and closure-day attribution. |
